Privacy Policy

Our Commitment: Project Unmute is designed with privacy first. We process data on your device and do not collect, store, or share your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Project Unmute processes the following data locally on your device:

  • Camera Data: Video from your iPhone camera or Meta glasses is processed in real-time for hand gesture detection. This data is never stored or transmitted.
  • Audio Data: Speech is processed using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not recorded or stored.
  • Hand Landmark Data: Hand position coordinates are analyzed locally for ASL sign detection and immediately discarded.

2. How We Use Information

All data processing occurs entirely on your device for the sole purpose of:

  • Converting speech to ASL avatar demonstrations
  • Detecting ASL signs and converting them to text
  • Providing text-to-speech output

No data is sent to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services

Project Unmute integrates with:

  • Apple Speech Recognition: Uses Apple's on-device speech framework. Subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Meta Wearables SDK: If you use Ray-Ban Meta glasses, the connection is managed through Meta's SDK. Subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • MediaPipe: Google's hand detection runs entirely on-device with no data transmission.

4. Data Storage

Project Unmute does not store:

  • Video or images from your camera
  • Audio recordings
  • Transcriptions of your speech
  • History of detected signs
  • Any personal identifiers

5. Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties.

Since we don't collect data, there is nothing to share, sell, or transfer.

6. Children's Privacy

Project Unmute does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

7. Permissions Required

The app requests the following permissions, used only for core functionality:

  • Camera: To detect hand gestures for ASL recognition
  • Microphone: To capture speech for conversion to ASL
  • Speech Recognition: To transcribe speech to text
  • Bluetooth: To connect to Ray-Ban Meta glasses (optional)

8. Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. You can revoke app permissions at any time in your iPhone Settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact Us
