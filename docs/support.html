🤟 Project Unmute

Real-time ASL Translation App

📱 Getting Started

How do I use the app?

Project Unmute offers two modes:

  • Speech → ASL: Tap the microphone and speak. Watch the avatar demonstrate ASL signs.
  • ASL → Text: Select a camera source and sign in ASL. Your signs will be converted to text and spoken aloud.

Which camera should I use?

  • iPhone Front Camera: Best for selfie-mode signing and demos
  • iPhone Back Camera: For third-person recording
  • Ray-Ban Meta Glasses: Hands-free first-person view (requires Meta glasses)

🕶️ Meta Glasses Setup

How do I connect Ray-Ban Meta glasses?

  1. Install the Meta AI app on your iPhone
  2. Pair your Ray-Ban Meta glasses via Bluetooth in the Meta AI app
  3. Enable Developer Mode in Meta AI settings
  4. Open Project Unmute and tap "Meta Glasses" as your camera source
  5. Authorize when prompted in Meta AI

Do I need Meta glasses to use the app?

No! The app works fully with your iPhone's front or back camera. Meta glasses are optional for a hands-free experience.

🤔 Troubleshooting

Signs aren't being detected

  • Ensure good lighting on your hands
  • Keep your hand within the camera frame
  • Hold signs steady for 1-2 seconds
  • Check that camera permissions are granted

Speech recognition isn't working

  • Check microphone permissions in Settings → Project Unmute
  • Ensure you have an internet connection (required for speech recognition)
  • Speak clearly and at a normal pace

"No devices found" for Meta Glasses

  • Ensure glasses are paired in the Meta AI app first
  • Update Meta AI app to the latest version
  • Try restarting both the app and your glasses

🌍 Supported Signs & Languages

Which ASL signs are supported?

Currently supported signs include:

  • Numbers: 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
  • Greetings: Hello, Bye, Thank You, Please, Sorry, OK
  • Responses: Yes, Stop, Good
  • Gestures: Peace, I Love You

Which languages are supported for output?

Text-to-speech output is available in:

  • English
  • Spanish
  • Chinese (Mandarin)
